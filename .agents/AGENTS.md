# Global Writing Rules

## Banned Characters
- **Em Dash (U+2014)**: NEVER use the em dash (U+2014) symbol anywhere in the codebase, content files, UI, or artifacts. This symbol is a known footprint of AI-generated content and is considered a severe red flag. Use standard hyphens (`-`), colons (`:`), pipes (`|`), or restructure the sentence to avoid it entirely.
